# Design Guidelines – Notry Plataforma Web

## Design Context

### Users
Sales and operations managers tracking lot allocation and inventory distribution. They need to understand the breakdown of available, reserved, and sold units at a glance for business decisions and strategic planning.

### Brand Personality
Professional, refined, trustworthy. The interface should feel premium without being pretentious—think "sophisticated property management tool," not "corporate bloatware."

### Aesthetic Direction
**Warm, refined minimalism** with golden undertones. The palette centers on a golden accent (#c5a059) paired with amber (#f59e0b) and red (#ef4444) for status states. Use smooth transitions, subtle shadows, and generous whitespace. Typography leads with Space Grotesk for impact and Outfit for body text.

**Anti-references:** Bright neons, aggressive gradients, glassmorphism, or decorative blur. No AI-template card grids or repeated icon + text patterns.

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity through elegance** — Every component should feel intentional and refined; clarity comes from restraint, not noise.
2. **Warmth with precision** — Use the golden palette as a cohesive anchor; semantic colors (amber, red) communicate state without overwhelming.
3. **Respectful hierarchy** — Bold typography, generous spacing, and color contrast guide the eye; no competing visual elements.
4. **Smooth, purposeful motion** — Transitions use cubic-bezier easing and never animate layout properties; motion reinforces state changes.
5. **Context-aware density** — Dashboards show summary data at a glance; detailed tables provide depth when needed; no stacked nesting or redundant information.

## Component Guidelines

### Progress Bar (Lot Distribution)
- **Purpose:** Sales managers need to see the proportion of available, reserved, and sold lots at a glance.
- **Aesthetic:** Horizontal segmented bar using the warm palette (golden for available, amber for reserved, red for sold).
- **Detail:** Show counts and percentages on hover or always visible—let users understand exact distribution.
- **Context:** Replaces three separate stat cards; lives at the top of the inventory view for maximum visibility.

### Color Semantics
- **Available:** #c5a059 (golden—primary, aspirational)
- **Reserved:** #f59e0b (amber—transitional, in-progress)
- **Sold:** #ef4444 (red—complete, terminal)

## Design Context

### Users
**Lote owners** (land developers) and **advisors** (sales agents) who manage real estate inventory. Mixed usage: desk-based power users at office and field agents on mobile. Users are managing land lot sales — a high-value, professional B2B context. They need speed, clarity, and confidence when updating lot status, pricing, and prospect information.

### Brand Personality
**Premium, warm, grounded** — not cold or corporate. The platform should feel like a trusted tool that respects the user's expertise and time. Warm cream backgrounds (#faf8f4) with gold accents (#c5a059) communicate approachability without sacrificing professionalism. Voice: direct, confident, no filler language.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Warm editorial utility** — inspired by high-end real estate brochures and premium SaaS dashboards. Space Grotesk (headings) + Outfit (body) typography pair. Warm off-white backgrounds, clean white surface cards, gold accent throughout. Not brutalist, not playful — restrained elegance with purposeful decoration.

### Design Principles
1. **Modal-first for writes, table for reads** — viewing data is table-based; creating and editing always opens a modal. Never inline-edit in tables. This keeps the table scannable and the edit context focused.
2. **Token everything** — no hardcoded color values, spacing numbers, or animation durations. Always use CSS variables from `ui/tokens/index.css`.
3. **Action buttons deserve weight** — 32px touch targets (--btn-height-sm), consistent 4px radius, icon size 16px. Hover and focus states are immediate and confident.
4. **Forms are serious work** — form rows use `var(--space-md)` gap; labels are uppercase with letter-spacing; focus rings use the gold accent color.
5. **Deliberate whitespace** — rhythm matters. Not everything needs to breathe equally. Dense data tables, generous section separation.
